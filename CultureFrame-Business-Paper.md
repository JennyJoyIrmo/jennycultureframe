
### a. Value Proposition

The team developed CultureFrame to address the critical need for cultural heritage preservation in Mansalay, Oriental Mindoro. For local communities, the platform provides digital preservation of their cultural heritage while creating economic opportunities through an integrated e-commerce system. Community members can document and share their cultural knowledge while artisans can showcase and sell their traditional crafts to a broader audience.

For tourists and cultural enthusiasts, CultureFrame offers a comprehensive guide to authentic cultural experiences, providing direct access to local products, services, and cultural sites. The platform displays detailed information about heritage sites, cultural events, and local attractions, enabling visitors to plan meaningful cultural experiences.

The platform serves educational institutions by providing rich cultural content for curriculum development, interactive learning materials, and research resources that support academic programs focused on cultural studies and heritage preservation. The app can display heritage site ratings, location information, and event schedules. With these available features, users can choose the right cultural experiences for them.

---

### b. Customer Segments

Based on the cultural preservation needs assessment conducted by the team, the target customers include local residents and community members in Mansalay who are interested in preserving and sharing their cultural heritage. Local artisans and craftspeople represent a key segment seeking online market presence to expand their customer base beyond the local area.

Tourists and cultural enthusiasts, both domestic and international, form another important segment looking for authentic cultural experiences and direct access to local products. Educational institutions and researchers studying cultural heritage constitute the tertiary segment requiring comprehensive cultural resources and documentation.

### c. Channels

The channels represent where cultural engagement and transactions occur. In CultureFrame, the following channels facilitate user interaction:

 Website  in here, details and description of the platform and cultural content will be presented; comprehensive heritage documentation and marketplace features are accessible through the web interface;

 FAQ  frequently asked questions will be included in the website, users can also send inquiries to the team through the contact form;

 Web Application  this is where available heritage sites, products, and events will appear, or the actual platform of the business; users, including community members, tourists, and artisans, must access this to engage with cultural content and marketplace features;

 Digital  via smartphone, tablet, or desktop with responsive design ensuring seamless access across all devices.

### d. Relationship

This defines the engagement of the users or customers of this platform. The relationship strategies include:

- By doing business through the e-commerce marketplace where artisans sell products and tourists make purchases;

- Their active participation, including rating heritage sites, sharing cultural moments, and contributing user-generated content;

- By collaboration, such as spreading awareness about cultural preservation, sharing the platform with other community members, and participating in cultural events;

- Through community engagement features, users will feel more connected and involved with the platform and the cultural preservation mission, fostering a sense of ownership and pride in their heritage.

### e. Key Partners

The key partners of CultureFrame are selected for their direct contribution to cultural preservation and platform success. They provide expertise, funding, and implementation support while helping to promote the platform. They are the following:

- Educational institutions and universities for curriculum integration and academic partnerships;

- Local artisan cooperatives and businesses for marketplace content and economic development;

### i. Revenue Streams

This component encompasses the money that CultureFrame generates from each defined customer segment. The revenue streams include:

**Primary Revenue Streams:**

**1. E-commerce Commission (40% of total revenue)**
- 5-10% commission on product sales from local artisans and craftspeople
- Transaction fees for payment processing through the platform
- Premium seller accounts with enhanced visibility, analytics, and promotional features

**2. Event Management Fees (25% of total revenue)**
- Event registration and ticketing fees (5-10% per ticket sold)
- Premium event promotion services for event organizers
- Sponsored event listings and featured placements on the platform
- Workshop and cultural activity booking fees

**3. Advertising Revenue (20% of total revenue)**
- Banner advertisements on platform pages for tourism operators and local businesses
- Sponsored content and featured product listings
- Tourism operator partnerships and promotional packages
- Featured artisan and business placements in search results and homepage

**4. Subscription Services (10% of total revenue)**
- Premium user accounts with additional features (unlimited photo uploads, advanced search)
- Business accounts for artisans with enhanced visibility and analytics tools
- Educational institution licenses for curriculum access and research resources
- Advanced analytics and reporting tools for businesses and researchers

**5. Consulting and Services (5% of total revenue)**
- Cultural heritage digitization services for other municipalities and regions
- Platform customization and white-label solutions for other communities
- Training and workshop services for cultural preservation and digital documentation
- Technical consulting for heritage preservation projects and digital archiving
