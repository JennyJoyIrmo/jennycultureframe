# CultureFrame Digital Heritage Platform

**Preserving Heritage, Empowering Communities**

## 🌿 Welcome to CultureFrame

Discover the rich cultural heritage of Mansalay, Oriental Mindoro through our comprehensive digital platform. CultureFrame connects communities, preserves traditions, and creates opportunities for local artisans and businesses.

**Explore Our Rich History**

**Features:**
- Interactive heritage galleries
- Historical documentation  
- Cultural storytelling
- Educational resources
